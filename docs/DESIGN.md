# Design context

Soko helps people discover things to do nearby. This exercise covers only discovery,
event details and saved plans, with a fixed Lisbon weekend and synthetic content.
The starter is inspired by Soko, not a pixel-perfect reproduction of the current app.

Use `lib/theme.dart` for the palette: paper `#F9F0F0`, ink `#44131D`, pink `#FFB8CB`,
yellow `#F0F288`, blue `#8BDFFF`, green `#B0EF8B`, lilac `#C588F2`.
`SokoButton` provides a shared pink primary action. Illustrations are drawn locally
in `EventArtwork`; no image service or licensed production font is needed.

You may evolve layout, hierarchy, copy and interactions to support your chosen
improvements. Explain your choices. There is no requirement for access to Figma or
Soko's internal design system.
